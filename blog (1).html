Trends

Why so many people are trading sedans for SUVs

It isn't really about size. The reasons people give point somewhere more specific.

Read
Across early submissions, the most repeated reason for choosing an SUV over a sedan isn't cargo space, it's seating height. People consistently describe wanting to sit higher in traffic, see further ahead, and feel less boxed in on the highway. Fuel economy comes up as a concern but rarely as a dealbreaker, since most modern SUVs have closed that gap. The second most common reason is resale value, several drivers noted they chose a popular SUV specifically because it would be easy to sell in a few years. What's mostly absent is any mention of off-roading or towing, suggesting the shift is more about everyday comfort than capability.

EVs

What actually convinces someone to go electric

Not the environment. Not always the cost. Something more mundane.

Read
The single most common reason drivers gave for switching to an EV was simply not wanting to stop for gas anymore, described almost identically across dozens of unrelated submissions. Charging at home overnight came up far more than public charging or road-trip range, which suggests most early EV buyers are people with a driveway or garage rather than street parking. Environmental reasons were mentioned, but usually as a secondary point after the daily convenience argument. Cost savings appeared too, but with more hedging, several people noted it depends heavily on local electricity prices.

First cars

What people actually wish they'd known before their first car

Almost nobody mentions the sticker price.

Read
Looking at entries from people describing their first car, the regret that comes up most isn't the purchase price, it's insurance cost, which several people said they hadn't properly researched before buying. The second most common note was about parts availability and repair cost for less common models, a handful of drivers said they'd pick something more common if they were doing it again simply because repairs were cheaper and faster. Very few mentioned wishing they'd bought something faster or flashier, most of the regret was practical rather than aspirational.
