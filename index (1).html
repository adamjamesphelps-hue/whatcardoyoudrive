05 — PARTNERS

For car companies

The record, turned into something you can act on.

Every entry on this site is a real person explaining, in their own words, why they chose the car they drive. That's the part surveys and clinics struggle to get honestly, and it's happening here continuously, for free, at growing scale.

We turn that into aggregated, anonymized insight: which models are winning cross-shop decisions, what real owners say drove the choice, and how that shifts month to month by body type and segment. No individual entry is ever sold or shared privately, everything here is already public. What you'd license is the pattern across thousands of them.

Trend reportsMonthly reads on why specific models are winning or losing cross-shop decisions.
Competitive readsWhat your owners say about you vs. named competitors, unprompted.
Early signalShifts in reasoning often show up here before they show up in sales data.
